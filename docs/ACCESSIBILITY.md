# Accessibility statement

Affreso aims to meet **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

## What we do

- Semantic HTML, with every form field labelled.
- A logical heading order and landmark regions. A "skip to main content" link appears on every page.
- Visible keyboard focus. Every action works with a keyboard alone, and pages work without JavaScript.
- Text and badge colours meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
- Responsive layouts from 320 px phones upwards. Text can be zoomed to 200%.
- Animations are reduced when "reduce motion" is set.
- Right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew.
- Status is never shown by colour alone: badges always include words.

## How it was tested

Versions 2.0 and 3.0 were checked with **axe-core 4** (rule sets wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa and best practice). The test covered public pages and pages for administrators, pastors and volunteers, including the dashboard, people, rosters, calendar, check-in, services, library, prayer, reimbursements, courses, giving, reports and settings. No violations remained.

Automated tools find only part of the barriers people meet. Before relying on Affreso for people with disabilities, churches should also test with a screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) and keyboard only.

## Kiosk

The kiosk has large touch targets and a number keypad, and also works with a hardware keyboard. It resets after a period of inactivity rather than on a fixed timer, so nobody is interrupted partway through.

## Known limitations

- The drawn signature pad needs a pointer. Typing your name is always enough to sign.
- Check-in labels are designed for printing and are not meant to be read on screen.

## Feedback

If something is hard to use, tell your church administrator so they can pass it on.
