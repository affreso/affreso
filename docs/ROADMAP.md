# Affreso roadmap

## Done

- **Version 1.0:** core modules.
- **Version 2.0:**
  - children's check-in
  - incidents
  - service planning
  - resource library and e-signatures
  - reimbursements
  - regular giving and statements
  - prayer
  - multi-campus
  - multilingual extras
  - accessibility
  - installable app
  - integrations
- **Version 3.0:**
  - repeating events
  - attendance headcounts
  - self check-in kiosk
  - two-way Google / Microsoft calendar sync
  - direct Elvanto / Planning Center import
  - encrypted push notifications with content
  - Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean translations
- **Version 4.0:**
  - online forms builder with optional payment
  - pledges and campaigns
  - pre-check-in from the phone
  - direct label printing through PrintNode
  - Vietnamese, Arabic and Persian translations
- **Version 4.1:**
  - Arabic and Persian names print correctly on server-drawn labels
  - paying for forms in monthly instalments
  - form answers that update profiles
- **Version 4.2:**
  - refunds from the response page (to the card through Stripe, or recorded as cash or bank)
  - paid event registration, with instalments, through a linked form
- **Version 4.3:**
  - refunds for gifts (to the card through Stripe, or recorded), reflected in giving statements
  - ticket types on forms, including family tickets that count as several places
